# Privacy Policy

madmd does not collect, store, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, advertising, or server of its own.

## Your files

Documents are opened, previewed, and saved only on your Mac. Nothing you open in madmd or preview in Quick Look is sent anywhere by madmd.

## Full Previews

In the App Store version, you can turn on Full Previews to show images, audio, video, and subtitles that a document links from other folders. A small helper then runs in the background while you are logged in. It reads only those media types, only from the folders you choose, and only to show them in madmd and Quick Look. The list of folders stays on your Mac. You can change it or turn Full Previews off at any time in Settings.

## Network

madmd connects to the internet only when a document asks for it:

- remote images in a document are loaded from the addresses the document gives;
- maps for GeoJSON and TopoJSON load background tiles from OpenStreetMap.

These requests go directly to those servers, which see your IP address as with any web request. madmd sends no other data with them.
